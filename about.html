About Sam

Twenty years in financial services

Sam has spent over two decades in financial services, working across development banking, auto finance and deposit mobilisation before specialising in mortgage and equity release advice. He is based in Cambridge and takes on clients across the UK.

He is a whole-of-market adviser, meaning he can recommend products from across the lending market rather than a restricted panel. He holds the Certificate in Mortgage Advice and Practice (CeMAP) and the Certificate in Regulated Equity Release (CeRER), alongside an MBA from Edith Cowan University.

Sam advises on first-time buyer mortgages, remortgages and product transfers, buy-to-let and commercial lending, further advances, equity release, and protection cover.

A mortgage is usually the largest commitment a person takes on, and the process is more complicated than it needs to be. Sam's approach is to lay out the options plainly, be clear about what each one costs, and give clients enough information to make the decision themselves.
