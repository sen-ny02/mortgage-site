About

Twenty years in financial services

Sam has spent over two decades in financial services, working across development banking, auto finance and deposit mobilisation before specialising in mortgage and equity release advice.

He is a whole-of-market adviser, meaning he can recommend products from across the lending market rather than a restricted panel. He holds the Certificate in Mortgage Advice and Practice (CeMAP) and the Certificate in Regulated Equity Release (CeRER), alongside an MBA from Edith Cowan University.

Sam advises on first-time buyer mortgages, remortgages and product transfers, buy-to-let and commercial lending, further advances, equity release, and protection cover. He is based in Cambridge and takes on clients across the UK.

How Sam works

What to expect from working with an independent, whole-of-market adviser.

  • Whole of market

    Sam is not tied to a panel of lenders. He can look across the whole market and compare products on their merits.

  • Plain English

    Mortgage and equity release terms can be confusing. Sam explains what each option means and what it costs, without jargon.

  • No pressure

    The first conversation is about understanding your situation. You decide whether and when to move forward.

What happens after you get in touch

A first conversation is free and there is no obligation to proceed.

  1. Introductory call

    A short call to understand what you're trying to do and answer any initial questions.

  2. Fact find

    Sam gathers the detail on your income, outgoings and circumstances needed to research the market properly.

  3. Recommendation

    You receive a written recommendation setting out the options considered and why.

  4. Application

    If you decide to proceed, Sam manages the application through to completion and keeps you updated.
