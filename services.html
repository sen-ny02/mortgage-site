What I can help with

Mortgage and protection services

Whole-of-market advice across residential and commercial lending, plus the protection cover that sits alongside it. Every recommendation is researched across the whole market rather than a restricted panel.

First-time buyers

Working out what you can borrow, what deposit you need, and which lenders will consider your situation. Sam looks at your income, outgoings and circumstances to identify the range of options realistically available before comparing products across the whole market.

This includes explaining schemes such as guarantor and family-assisted mortgages, and what a Decision in Principle is used for.

Remortgaging

Reviewing your current deal before it ends, and comparing a product transfer from your existing lender against moving to a new one. Timing matters here — Sam can flag when it's worth starting the conversation, usually a few months before your current deal expires.

Sam checks whether your circumstances have changed since you last borrowed, and what that means for the options available to you now.

Buy-to-let

Advice for landlords, including portfolio lending and limited company structures. This covers individual landlords letting a single property as well as those building a wider portfolio.

Specialist lenders assess buy-to-let applications differently from a residential mortgage, usually against the property's likely rental income rather than your personal salary alone.

Commercial lending

Finance for business premises and commercial investment property. This includes owner-occupied premises, commercial investment purchases, and further advances against existing commercial assets.

Commercial lending is assessed on a more individual basis than residential mortgages, so early conversation with a lender is usually worthwhile.

Equity release

Later-life lending options that let you access value tied up in your home without moving. Sam sets out how each product works, what it costs over the life of the plan, and how it can affect the value of your estate and any means-tested benefits.

Equity release is a significant, often irreversible decision. Alternatives are always discussed alongside it, so you have the full picture before deciding anything.

Protection

Life cover, critical illness and income protection to sit alongside your mortgage. This covers protecting the mortgage itself, as well as wider family income needs if you were unable to work.

Cover can be arranged at the same time as a mortgage application or reviewed separately at any point.
